All 14 AUROCs cross-checked against scikit-learn within 1e-12. The complete
official preprocessing modules and the restricted reviewed-definition wrappers
produced identical cleaned strings on **all 600 inputs per publisher**.

Interpretation: cleanup changes the point estimates slightly, but does not resolve
the weak separation after expert editing. On like-for-like scores, edited-text
AUROC changes by +0.0120 for MAGE softmax, +0.0144 for MAGE logit and +0.0146 for
SuperAnnotate sigmoid. These descriptive changes do not establish statistical
superiority, interview performance or a deployable operating threshold.

MAGE's publisher threshold uses class-0 raw logits; switching score representations
can change ordering because softmax also depends on the other class's logit. A
threshold change alone cannot improve AUROC. No threshold is adopted here.
